FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the store (skins) panel on the Game's title screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account) you bought with.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them for a bonus. If interstitials still appear, try Restore Purchases, then restart the Game.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with steps to reproduce it and a screenshot if possible.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple or Google Play.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Use Privacy Options in the Game's Settings. See our Privacy Policy.

Sach'a Amaru's Doctrine

Need help with Sach'a Amaru's Doctrine for iPad? Email cananope1+support@gmail.com with your iPad model, iPadOS version, game version (Settings › About) and the chapter or card where it happened. I aim to reply within 3 business days. The Game is developed and sold by Andres Cabrera.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPad. The Game checkpoints at the start of each chapter; if you die, you return to the chapter start with your story intact. Deleting the Game deletes your saves.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. It's a one-time purchase with no ads and no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to restore.

How many endings are there?

Ten: eight early endings when one of the four forces breaks, an escape at dawn, and a hidden true ending.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

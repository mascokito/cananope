Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy covers The Gilded Boy (the "Game") by Cananope Softworks ("we"). We do not run accounts or servers for the Game, and we do not collect your name, email, or contact details through it.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, purchases unlocked and high scores (including the leaderboard) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. You can delete them by uninstalling the Game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded ads (that you choose to watch) and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID), device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location derived from your IP address, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

  • Consent: We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), ads are personalized only if you consent; otherwise non-personalized ads are shown.
  • iOS tracking: On iPhone and iPad, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks your permission before the IDFA is used. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Changing your choices: Use Privacy Options in the Game's Settings, when shown, to review or withdraw consent.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card or billing details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so the Game can unlock it.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us.

US state privacy rights

Residents of California and other US states may have rights to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Ad data shared with Google may count as "sharing". To opt out, use Privacy Options in the Game's Settings (the "Do Not Sell or Share" choice) and/or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

EEA/UK rights (GDPR)

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (via Privacy Options), and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are consent (personalized ads) and legitimate interests (non-personalized ads, fraud prevention). Since we hold no data about you, most requests are handled through Google; contact us and we will help.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date.
